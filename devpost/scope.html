Skincare Routine
Personaliser

A personal skincare app that uses the products someone owns and their selected skin concerns to suggest routines they can save and follow.

Approved scope
The unique kernel

Make your own products fit a routine.

Suggestions use a person's selected skin concerns and products they already own, with possible cosmetic results to help compare routines.

Who it's for

Someone with a skincare stash

Initially, someone like the learner: they own a number of products, want informed choices, and can feel overwhelmed deciding what to use. They want more consistency and currently search online when uncertain.

The core loop

Questionnaire → suggestions from the small product set → choose → saved routine

Why this matters

Less overwhelm, more consistency

The learner wants to make better use of skincare they already own and understand how planning leads toward production-ready apps.

What “working” looks like

One suggested routine is saved

A person selects concerns and products, shares schedule preferences, compares suggestions, chooses one, and sees an ordered routine in their profile on the same device.

Scope boundary

One questionnaire-to-routine flow, built around a small product set.

Product guidance takes priority for timing and frequency when available; otherwise the app uses the person's preferences. Suggested products appear in the learner's stated sequence: serums, moisturiser, then sunscreen. If no suggestion fits, they can adjust or reduce selected skin issues.

Now In this proof of concept
  • Phone-first, minimal design with generous white space.
  • Questionnaire for skin issues, products owned from a preloaded set, and schedule preferences.
  • Suggested routines based on selected issues and owned products, with possible cosmetic results and products ordered as serums, moisturiser, then sunscreen.
  • Product usage information guides timing/frequency where available; user preference fills gaps.
  • Choose one suggestion and save the routine on the current device.
  • If no match appears, adjust the questionnaire or select fewer issues.
Later Deferred for now
  • Manually creating routines or adding a product to an existing routine.
  • Swap suggestions, reserve items, and tracking items as finished or expired.
  • Accounts and access across devices.
  • Broader product recognition or recommendations for products the person does not own.
  • More complete product analysis and additional cosmetic concerns or results.
Cut Explicitly ruled out

No feature has been ruled out permanently. These items are deferred rather than rejected.

Why this revision?

The demo now centers on getting a suggested routine from the person's concerns and products, then saving one to revisit. Manual routine edits, product swaps, reserve tracking, and cross-device accounts are deferred to keep this first experiment focused.
