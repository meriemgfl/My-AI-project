Devpost Learn · Product requirements · Working title

Skincare Routine
Personaliser

A phone-first app for someone with skincare products who wants less-overwhelming routines based on their concerns and products.

Approved PRD

The main journey

A suggestion uses selected concerns and owned-product information; expiry and product guidance shape the result.

Who it serves

Someone with a skincare stash

They want to make informed choices and be more consistent without getting overwhelmed or restarting online research each time.

Design direction

Minimal and spacious

Phone-first, with generous white space, guided by the learner's reference image. Final colors and typography remain open.

Scope boundary

Questionnaire → suggested routine → saved routine

Manual routine creation, direct product swaps, barcode scanning, and cross-device accounts are deferred. The product list uses a small preloaded set.

Screens and behavior

1 Home and saved routines
  • Show saved routine cards with day/night, clock time, weekly frequency, and target concern(s).
  • Offer one Create a routine action, which opens the questionnaire and collects profile details.
  • Offer a separate View/create product list action. No duplicate Finish Profile button.
  • On first use, show those actions even though there are no saved routines.
Acceptance checks
  • First visit shows the two actions and no fabricated saved routines.
  • A selected suggestion appears on the home screen and can be opened later.
2 Product list
  • Browse or search the preloaded set and select owned items for the personal list.
  • Show name, brand, ingredients, estimated price, benefits, and expiration date.
  • Enter the expiration date for the person's own item. If it is unknown, keep the product eligible and show a “Missing expiry date” label. Barcode scanning is out of scope.
  • Reuse saved products in the next questionnaire.
Acceptance checks
  • Products can be found through search or browsing and added to the list.
  • Product details show the agreed fields.
  • Expired products are absent from routine suggestions; products with no known date remain eligible and show a “Missing expiry date” label.
3 Questionnaire
  • Choose multiple concerns from suggestions, including all examples named: dry skin, aging skin, fine lines, rosacea, and sun damage.
  • Select products from the saved product list; start with saved products already selected.
  • Set preferred day/night, clock time, and number of uses per week.
Acceptance checks
  • More than one concern can be selected.
  • Saved products are preselected for a returning person.
  • Schedule preferences are available when product use information is missing.
4 Suggested routines
  • Show potential routines using both selected concerns and product information.
  • Display target concern(s), possible results from product information, schedule, and ordered steps.
  • Product guidance takes priority for timing/frequency; use the person's preference when information is absent.
  • Order supported categories using the learner's sequence: serums → moisturiser → sunscreen.
  • Exclude expired items. If no suggestion fits, let the person adjust answers or choose fewer concerns; don't suggest products they don't own.
  • Choosing one saves it to the profile on the current device.
Acceptance checks
  • Suggestions reflect the selected concerns and owned products.
  • Possible results trace to product information.
  • Steps show the expected order and schedule precedence.
  • No-match state offers a way to adjust answers.
  • Chosen routine persists after reopening the app on that device.
5 Routine details
  • Show target concerns and schedule.
  • Show numbered product steps with a short purpose/use instruction for each product.
Acceptance checks
  • A saved routine can be reopened and its target, schedule, products, and order are visible.

Decisions captured

  • All the concerns named by the learner are included, including rosacea.
  • Products with an unknown expiration date remain eligible and carry a “Missing expiry date” label.

What's sharp, and what's still open

The central journey and no-match behavior are clear, and the routine can be checked by looking at its products, order, schedule, and saved state. The main journey and the key product rules are now concrete: concern selection, product information, expiry handling, routine order, and same-device saving. The remaining details, such as a final app name and exact visual styling, can be settled during the build.
